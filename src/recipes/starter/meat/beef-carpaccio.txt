Beef Carpaccio

Ingredients

200 g beef tenderloin
2 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp lemon juice
30 g Parmesan cheese
1 handful of arugula
Salt
Freshly ground black pepper

Instructions
Wrap the beef tenderloin tightly in plastic wrap.

Place it in the freezer for about 30–45 minutes to make it easier to slice.

Remove the beef from the freezer and slice it very thinly.

Arrange the beef slices on serving plates.

Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice.

Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper.

Add arugula on top.

Shave or grate Parmesan cheese over the beef.

Serve immediately.

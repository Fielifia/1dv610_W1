Steak Tartare

Ingredients

250 g beef tenderloin
1 small shallot, finely chopped
1 tbsp capers, finely chopped
1 tsp Dijon mustard
1 egg yolk
1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
1 tbsp olive oil
Salt
Black pepper
Fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions
Make sure the beef is very fresh and suitable for eating raw.

Finely chop the beef with a sharp knife.

Place the beef in a bowl.

Add the shallot, capers, Dijon mustard and Worcestershire sauce.

Add the olive oil and mix gently.

Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper.

Carefully mix in the egg yolk.

Add fresh parsley.

Shape the tartare into a neat portion using a small mould or spoon.

Serve immediately.

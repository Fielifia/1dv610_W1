Mini Beef Meatballs

Ingredients

300 g ground beef
½ yellow onion, finely chopped
1 garlic clove, minced
1 egg
2 tbsp breadcrumbs
½ tsp salt
½ tsp black pepper
1 tbsp butter or olive oil

Instructions
Add the ground beef, onion, garlic, egg and breadcrumbs to a bowl.

Season with salt and black pepper.

Mix everything together until well combined.

Shape the mixture into small meatballs.

Heat butter or olive oil in a frying pan.

Fry the meatballs over medium heat for about 8–10 minutes.

Turn them regularly so they cook evenly.

Make sure the meatballs are fully cooked.

Serve warm as a starter.

Tuna with Parmesan Mayonnaise

Ingredients
Serves 4

Parmesan Mayonnaise
1 dl mayonnaise
1 dl Parmesan cheese
1 tsp lemon juice
Salt

Chimichurri
1/2 small shallot
1/2 red chili
1/2 clove garlic, grated
1/2 bunch fresh parsley
1/2 bunch fresh coriander/cilantro, plus leaves for topping
0.75 dl olive oil
1/2 lemon, juice and zest
Salt and black pepper

Tuna
1–2 tomatoes, depending on size
150 g fresh tuna
2 slices white sandwich bread, preferably sourdough
Plenty of butter for frying

Instructions

Parmesan Mayonnaise
Mix all the ingredients together.

Chimichurri
Finely chop the shallot, chili, garlic, parsley and coriander/cilantro.

Mix with the olive oil, lemon juice and lemon zest.

Season with salt and black pepper.

Tuna
Dice the tomatoes.

Cut the tuna into small pieces and place in a bowl.

Add a couple of tablespoons of chimichurri and mix. Season with salt and black pepper.

Remove the crusts from the bread and cut the slices into long, narrow pieces.

Fry the bread over medium heat in plenty of butter.

Fry until golden brown on all sides.

To Serve
Spread Parmesan mayonnaise over the toasted bread.

Top with the diced tomatoes and tuna.

Drizzle with more chimichurri and finish with fresh coriander/cilantro.

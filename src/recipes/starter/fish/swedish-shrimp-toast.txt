Swedish Shrimp Toast

Ingredients
Serves 4

1 kg shrimp with shells (preferably fresh, high-quality shrimp)
2 egg yolks
2 tsp mustard
1 tbsp white wine vinegar
6 dl neutral cooking oil
1 bunch fresh dill
10 cm fresh horseradish, peeled
4 slices white bread (not sourdough/levain)
Butter, for frying
50 g vendace roe
1 lemon

Instructions
Peel all the shrimp and set aside.

Make a mayonnaise by placing the egg yolks, mustard, and white wine vinegar in a bowl. Slowly add the oil in a thin, steady stream while whisking continuously. Use an electric or hand whisk. The mayonnaise is ready when it is thick enough that the whisk leaves visible trails in it.

Place the shrimp in a bowl. Finely chop the dill and add it to the shrimp. Gradually stir in the mayonnaise until the shrimp are evenly coated.

Grate in a little horseradish and taste. Add more mayonnaise for a looser mixture or more horseradish for a stronger kick.

Cut the bread into your desired shape, removing the crusts. You can use a bowl or glass as a template if you want round pieces of toast. Fry the bread in butter until golden brown.

Place the toasted bread on plates. Top with the shrimp mixture and a generous spoonful of vendace roe.

Finish with a sprig of dill and a lemon slice.

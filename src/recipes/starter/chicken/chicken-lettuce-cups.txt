Chicken Lettuce Cups

Ingredients

300 g chicken breast, finely chopped
1 tbsp olive oil
1 clove garlic, minced
1 small carrot, finely chopped
2 spring onions, chopped
1 tbsp soy sauce
1 tsp honey
1 tsp sesame oil
1 head of lettuce
Sesame seeds, for serving

Instructions
Wash and separate the lettuce leaves.

Cut the chicken into very small pieces.

Heat the olive oil in a frying pan.

Add the chicken and cook until almost fully cooked.

Add the garlic and carrot and cook for a few minutes.

Add the spring onions.

Stir in the soy sauce, honey and sesame oil.

Cook for another 2–3 minutes.

Spoon the chicken mixture into the lettuce leaves.

Sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Serve immediately.

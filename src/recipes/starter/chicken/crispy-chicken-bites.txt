Crispy Chicken Bites

Ingredients

300 g chicken breast
1 egg
75 g breadcrumbs
50 g flour
1 tsp paprika
½ tsp garlic powder
Salt
Black pepper
Oil, for frying
Sweet chili sauce, for serving

Instructions
Cut the chicken breast into small bite-sized pieces.

Season the chicken with salt, pepper, paprika and garlic powder.

Put the flour in one bowl.

Beat the egg in a second bowl.

Put the breadcrumbs in a third bowl.

Coat each piece of chicken in flour, then egg, and finally breadcrumbs.

Heat oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat.

Fry the chicken pieces until golden and completely cooked.

Drain on kitchen paper.

Serve warm with sweet chili sauce.

Mini Chicken Skewers

Ingredients

300 g chicken breast
1 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp soy sauce
1 tsp honey
1 garlic clove, minced
½ tsp paprika
Salt and black pepper
Wooden skewers

Instructions
Cut the chicken breast into small bite-sized pieces.

Mix the olive oil, soy sauce, honey, garlic and paprika in a bowl.

Add the chicken and mix until it is well coated.

Cover the bowl and let the chicken marinate for at least 15 minutes.

Put the chicken pieces onto the wooden skewers.

Heat a frying pan over medium-high heat.

Cook the chicken skewers for about 8–10 minutes, turning them regularly.

Make sure the chicken is fully cooked before serving.

Serve warm.

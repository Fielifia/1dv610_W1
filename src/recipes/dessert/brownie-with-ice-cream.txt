Brownie with Ice Cream

Ingredients

150 g butter
200 g sugar
2 eggs
100 g flour
40 g cocoa powder
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 pinch salt
100 g dark chocolate, chopped
Vanilla ice cream, for serving

Instructions
Preheat the oven to 175°C.

Melt the butter in a saucepan and let it cool slightly.

Mix the melted butter and sugar in a bowl.

Add the eggs and vanilla extract and mix well.

Add the flour, cocoa powder and salt.

Stir until everything is well combined.

Fold in the chopped chocolate.

Pour the mixture into a baking pan lined with baking paper.

Bake for approximately 20–25 minutes.

Let the brownie cool for a few minutes.

Cut into squares.

Serve warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

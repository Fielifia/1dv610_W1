Strawberry Cheesecake Cups

Ingredients

200 g cream cheese
100 ml heavy cream
2 tbsp sugar
1 tsp vanilla extract
150 g strawberries
100 g digestive biscuits
30 g melted butter

Instructions
Crush the digestive biscuits into small crumbs.

Mix the biscuit crumbs with the melted butter.

Whip the heavy cream until it becomes light and fluffy.

In another bowl, mix the cream cheese, sugar and vanilla extract.

Gently fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture.

Cut the strawberries into small pieces.

Add a layer of biscuit crumbs to each glass.

Add a layer of the cheesecake mixture.

Add some strawberries on top.

Repeat the layers if desired.

Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.

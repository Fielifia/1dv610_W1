Vanilla Panna Cotta

Ingredients

500 ml heavy cream
50 g sugar
1 tsp vanilla extract
3 gelatin sheets
Fresh berries, for serving

Instructions
Soak the gelatin sheets in cold water for about 5 minutes.

Add the heavy cream, sugar and vanilla extract to a saucepan.

Heat gently until the mixture is hot, but do not let it boil.

Remove the saucepan from the heat.

Squeeze the excess water from the gelatin sheets.

Add the gelatin to the warm cream mixture.

Stir until the gelatin has completely dissolved.

Pour the mixture into small serving glasses or moulds.

Refrigerate for at least 4 hours until set.

Serve with fresh berries.

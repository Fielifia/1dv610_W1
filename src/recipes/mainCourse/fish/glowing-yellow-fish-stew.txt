Glowing Yellow Fish Stew

Ingredients
Serves 4

400 g cod fillet, pollock, haddock, or hoki
300 g salmon fillet
1/2 leek (about 150 g)
1/2 yellow onion
1 tbsp butter or olive oil
1 garlic clove
1 1/2 tsp tomato paste
1 tsp dried thyme
1 tsp dried basil
2 1/2 dl dry white wine
1 1/2 fish stock cubes
2 dl heavy cream
1 dl crème fraîche
2 dl water
1 packet saffron (0.5 g)
1 tsp salt
300 g shell-on shrimp
1 can mussels (150 g)

Instructions
Thaw the fish if using frozen fish. Rinse, trim, and slice the leek. Peel and chop the onion.

Melt the butter in a large pot or heavy-bottomed saucepan. Sauté the onion and leek (reserve a little leek for serving) for a couple of minutes, until softened and translucent.

Press in the garlic. Stir in the tomato paste, thyme, and basil. Cook briefly.

Add the wine and fish stock cubes. Simmer for a couple of minutes.

Stir in the heavy cream, crème fraîche, water, and saffron. Simmer for 15 minutes. Season with salt to taste.

Cut the fish into bite-sized pieces. Add to the soup and simmer for another 7 minutes.

Peel the shrimp. Add the shrimp and drained mussels to the soup.

Top with the reserved leek.

Serving suggestion
Heat through and serve immediately, preferably with bread and garlic mayonnaise.

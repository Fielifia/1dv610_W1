Crispy Rice Salad with Salmon, Avocado, and Sesame Dressing

Ingredients
Serves 4

4 dl cooked jasmine rice (preferably leftover rice from the day before)
2 tbsp crispy chili oil
1 tbsp rapeseed oil
Salt
2 avocados
1 cucumber
3 spring onions
1 bunch radishes
200 g edamame beans, thawed
4 salmon fillets
Oil, for frying

To serve
Toasted sesame seeds
Fresh coriander (cilantro)
Felix Dark Roasted Sesame Dressing

Instructions
Preheat the oven to 200°C (390°F).

Line a baking tray with parchment paper and spread the cooked rice over it. Add the crispy chili oil, rapeseed oil, and a little salt. Mix thoroughly and spread the rice out in as thin and even a layer as possible. Bake for approximately 35 minutes, stirring occasionally. The rice is ready when it is crispy and golden.

Peel and cut the avocados into wedges. Cut the cucumber into pieces and thinly slice the spring onions and radishes.

Prepare the edamame beans according to the package instructions, if necessary, and make sure they are fully thawed and drained.

Season the salmon fillets with salt and pepper. Heat a little oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Fry the salmon fillets on both sides until nicely browned on the outside and cooked through.

Mix the crispy rice with the vegetables and edamame beans. Divide between plates and serve with the salmon. Top with fresh coriander, toasted sesame seeds, and dark roasted sesame dressing.

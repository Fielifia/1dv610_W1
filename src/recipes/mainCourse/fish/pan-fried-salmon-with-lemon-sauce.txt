Pan-Fried Salmon with Lemon Sauce

Ingredients
Serves 4

900 g potatoes
1 lemon
2 1/2 dl cooking cream
1 tsp cornstarch
150 g haricots verts (green beans)
Approx. 550 g salmon fillet
1 tsp oil
Salt
Pepper

Instructions
Peel the potatoes and boil them in salted water.

Rinse the lemon and grate the zest.

Pour the cream into a saucepan. Whisk in the cornstarch and bring to a simmer. Cook for a few minutes, stirring continuously. Season with the lemon zest.

Trim the haricots verts and boil them in salted water for approximately 4 minutes. Drain.

Cut the salmon into 4 equal-sized pieces. Fry the salmon in the oil for approximately 4 minutes on each side. Season with salt and pepper.

Cut the lemon into wedges.

Serve the salmon with the potatoes, lemon sauce, lemon wedges, and green beans.

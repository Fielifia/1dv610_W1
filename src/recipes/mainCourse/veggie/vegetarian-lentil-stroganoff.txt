Vegetarian Lentil Stroganoff

Time: 30 min
Serves: 4

Ingredients

3 dl dried red lentils
1 yellow onion
1 tsp smoked paprika
2 dl plant-based cream
1 dl water
2 tbsp tomato paste
1 vegetable stock cube
1 tbsp soy sauce, preferably Japanese-style, e.g. Kikkoman
8 sun-dried tomatoes
2 carrots
Salt and black pepper
Oil for frying
4 servings of rice

Instructions
Cook the lentils according to the package instructions.

Finely chop the onion. Heat oil in a frying pan and sauté the onion until soft, together with the smoked paprika.

Add the cream and water. Whisk in the tomato paste, vegetable stock cube and soy sauce. Let the tomato sauce simmer for about 10 minutes.

Meanwhile, start cooking the rice according to the package instructions.

Once the lentils are cooked, add them to the tomato sauce and stir gently.

Chop the sun-dried tomatoes and add them to the sauce.

Grate the carrots directly into the sauce.

Simmer over low heat for about 5–10 minutes.

Season with salt and black pepper. If needed, add more water, cream or milk to reach your desired consistency.

Serve with rice and, preferably, a side salad.

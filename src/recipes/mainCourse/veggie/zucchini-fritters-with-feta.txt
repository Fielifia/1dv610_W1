Zucchini Fritters with Feta Cheese

Ingredients
Makes 8 fritters

350 g zucchini
3 spring onions
150 g feta cheese
1/2 dl finely chopped fresh dill
1 dl all-purpose flour
3 eggs
1/2 tsp salt
1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
Neutral oil, for frying

To serve
Plain cooking yogurt (10% fat)
Fresh garlic, pressed

Instructions
Coarsely grate the zucchini. Place it in a colander and squeeze out as much liquid as possible.

Trim and thinly slice the spring onions. Crumble the feta cheese. Finely chop the dill.

Whisk together the flour and eggs. Add the zucchini, spring onions, feta cheese, dill, salt, and pepper. Mix until evenly combined.

Fry fritters approximately 10 cm (4 inches) in diameter in plenty of oil for about 2 minutes per side, or until golden brown.

Transfer to paper towels to drain.

Serving suggestion
Serve the zucchini fritters with cooking yogurt mixed with freshly pressed garlic.

Vodka Pasta

Ingredients
Serves 4

3 garlic cloves
2 tbsp olive oil
1 dl tomato paste
1/2 tsp chili flakes
1/2 dl vodka
2 1/2 dl heavy cream
Salt
Black pepper
4 servings pasta (e.g. penne)

To serve
Shaved Parmesan
Fresh basil
Salad

Instructions
Peel and finely chop the garlic. Sauté it in the olive oil until lightly golden.

Add the tomato paste and chili flakes. Cook for another 5 minutes over medium heat.

Pour in the vodka and let it simmer for about 1 minute.

Stir in the heavy cream and let the sauce simmer until slightly thickened. Season with salt and black pepper.

Cook the pasta according to the package instructions. Drain, reserving 1–2 dl of pasta water.

Add the pasta to the sauce. Gradually add some of the reserved pasta water, stirring until you reach your desired consistency.

Top with shaved Parmesan and fresh basil.

Serving suggestion
Serve with a simple salad.

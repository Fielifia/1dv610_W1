Entrecôte with Béarnaise Sauce, Potato Wedges & Asparagus

Ingredients

2 entrecôte steaks
600 g potatoes
200 g asparagus
2 tbsp olive oil
Salt
Black pepper

Béarnaise Sauce
150 g butter
2 egg yolks
1 tbsp white wine vinegar
1 tbsp water
1 small shallot, finely chopped
1 tsp dried or fresh tarragon
Salt
Black pepper

Instructions

Potato Wedges
Preheat the oven to 220°C.

Wash the potatoes and cut them into wedges.

Place the potato wedges on a baking tray.

Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and black pepper.

Bake for about 30–40 minutes, turning them halfway through.

Steak
Take the steaks out of the refrigerator about 20–30 minutes before cooking.

Season both sides with salt and black pepper.

Heat a frying pan over high heat.

Add a little oil and place the steaks in the pan.

Cook for about 2–4 minutes on each side, depending on the desired doneness.

Let the steaks rest for a few minutes before serving.

Asparagus
Trim the ends of the asparagus.

Heat a little oil in a frying pan.

Fry the asparagus for about 5–7 minutes.

Season with salt and black pepper.

Béarnaise Sauce
Melt the butter in a saucepan and keep it warm.

Put the egg yolks, vinegar, water and shallot in a heatproof bowl.

Whisk over gentle heat until the mixture becomes slightly thick.

Slowly add the melted butter while continuously whisking.

Add the tarragon and season with salt and pepper.

Serve the steak with the béarnaise sauce, potato wedges and asparagus.

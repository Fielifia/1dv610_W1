Spaghetti Bolognese

Ingredients

400 g spaghetti
500 g ground beef
1 yellow onion, finely chopped
2 cloves garlic, minced
2 carrots, finely chopped
1 celery stalk, finely chopped
400 g crushed tomatoes
2 tbsp tomato paste
100 ml red wine (optional)
1 tsp dried oregano
1 tsp dried basil
2 tbsp olive oil
Salt
Black pepper
Parmesan cheese, for serving

Instructions
Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan.

Add the onion, carrots and celery.

Cook for about 5 minutes until the vegetables soften.

Add the garlic and cook for another minute.

Add the ground beef and fry until browned.

Stir in the tomato paste.

Add the crushed tomatoes and red wine if using.

Season with oregano, basil, salt and black pepper.

Let the sauce simmer over low heat for at least 20–30 minutes.

Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti according to the instructions on the package.

Drain the spaghetti when it is cooked.

Serve the Bolognese sauce over the spaghetti.

Top with grated Parmesan cheese.

Swedish Meatballs

Ingredients

500 g mixed ground meat (beef and pork)
1 yellow onion, finely grated
1 egg
100 ml heavy cream
50 ml breadcrumbs
1 tsp salt
2 pinches freshly ground white pepper
1 pinch freshly ground black pepper
Butter, for frying

Mashed Potatoes
800 g floury potatoes
200 ml milk
50–100 ml heavy cream
50 g butter
½–¾ tsp salt
1–2 pinches white pepper

Cream Sauce
2 tbsp concentrated veal stock
200 ml water
300 ml heavy cream
1 pinch Chinese soy sauce
Freshly ground black pepper

To Serve
Lingonberry jam
Pickled cucumber

Instructions

Meatballs
Finely grate the onion.

Add the ground meat, grated onion, egg, heavy cream and breadcrumbs to a bowl.

Season with salt, white pepper and black pepper.

Mix everything together until well combined.

Shape the mixture into small meatballs.

Heat butter in a frying pan over medium heat.

Fry the meatballs until golden brown and fully cooked, turning them regularly.

Mashed Potatoes
Peel the potatoes and cut them into smaller pieces.

Place the potatoes in a pot of salted water.

Boil for about 15–20 minutes, or until tender.

Drain the potatoes.

Mash the potatoes while they are still warm.

Heat the milk and heavy cream gently.

Add the warm milk mixture and butter to the potatoes.

Season with salt and white pepper.

Mix until smooth and creamy.

Cream Sauce
Add the veal stock and water to a saucepan.

Bring to a gentle simmer.

Add the heavy cream and soy sauce.

Let the sauce simmer for a few minutes until it thickens slightly.

Season with freshly ground black pepper.

Serve the sauce with the meatballs and mashed potatoes.

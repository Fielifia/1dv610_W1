Creamy Chicken Pasta

Ingredients

500 g chicken breast
300 g pasta
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 yellow onion, chopped
200 ml heavy cream
100 ml chicken stock
50 g grated Parmesan cheese
1 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp Italian seasoning
Salt
Black pepper
Fresh parsley, for serving

Instructions
Cut the chicken breast into bite-sized pieces.

Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.

Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan.

Add the chicken and season with salt and black pepper.

Cook until the chicken is golden and fully cooked.

Remove the chicken from the pan and set aside.

Add the onion and garlic to the same pan.

Cook for a few minutes until softened.

Add the heavy cream and chicken stock.

Stir in the Parmesan cheese and Italian seasoning.

Let the sauce simmer for a few minutes.

Add the chicken and cooked pasta to the sauce.

Mix everything together.

Serve with fresh parsley and extra Parmesan.

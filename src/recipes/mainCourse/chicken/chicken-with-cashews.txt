Chicken with Cashews

Ingredients

500 g chicken thigh fillets
2 cloves garlic, chopped
2 carrots, peeled and sliced
1 red bell pepper, cut into bite-sized pieces
3 spring onions, cut into 3 cm pieces
2 pak choi, roughly chopped
50 ml rapeseed oil

Sauce
75 ml oyster sauce
1 tbsp black bean sauce (or 2 tbsp hoisin sauce)
½ tbsp rice vinegar
3 tbsp muscovado sugar (or brown sugar)
1 tbsp sesame oil

To serve
200 g cashew nuts
Jasmine rice

Instructions
Cut the chicken thigh fillets into bite-sized pieces.

Chop the garlic and prepare the vegetables.

Mix the oyster sauce, black bean sauce, rice vinegar, muscovado sugar and sesame oil in a bowl.

Heat the rapeseed oil in a large frying pan or wok over high heat.

Add the chicken and stir-fry for several minutes until golden and almost fully cooked.

Add the garlic, carrots and red bell pepper.

Stir-fry for a few minutes until the vegetables begin to soften.

Add the spring onions and pak choi.

Pour the sauce over the chicken and vegetables.

Stir everything together and cook for another 2–3 minutes.

Add the cashew nuts and mix well.

Make sure the chicken is completely cooked before serving.

Serve hot with jasmine rice.

Chicken Tikka Masala with Rice

Ingredients

500 g chicken breast
200 g basmati rice
1 yellow onion, chopped
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
400 g crushed tomatoes
200 ml coconut milk
2 tbsp tomato paste
1 tbsp olive oil
2 tsp curry powder
1 tsp paprika
1 tsp ground cumin
½ tsp chili powder
Salt
Black pepper
Fresh coriander, for serving

Instructions
Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.

Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan.

Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.

Remove the chicken from the pan and set aside.

Add the onion to the same pan and cook until softened.

Add the garlic and ginger and cook for about 1 minute.

Add the curry powder, paprika, cumin and chili powder.

Stir in the tomato paste.

Add the crushed tomatoes and coconut milk.

Stir well and bring the sauce to a gentle simmer.

Add the chicken back to the pan.

Simmer for about 15–20 minutes until the chicken is completely cooked.

Meanwhile, cook the basmati rice according to the package instructions.

Season the tikka masala with salt and black pepper.

Serve the chicken tikka masala with basmati rice.

Garnish with fresh coriander.
